Innovation Showcase Overview
The NCCU OeL Fall Innovation Showcase is a virtual event designed to equip faculty with practical tools that streamline scholarly workflows, reduce cognitive load, and open new avenues for research and publication. Three concurrent 20-minute sessions run across three time slots — offering variety without asking faculty to block an entire morning.
Session Schedule
10:00 – 10:20 AM
Research & Publication Tools
Annotate, Review, Collaborate
Tools for marking up drafts, collecting feedback, and sharing research documents — no AI required.
Mapping Your Research Journey
Citation managers to organize sources and build literature maps from scratch.
From Notes to Manuscript
Notion, OneNote & structured writing workflows for moving from raw notes to a polished white paper or publication.
10:30 – 10:50 AM
Stress Relief & Time-Saving Techniques
Reclaim Your Week
Scheduling tools to automate repetitive faculty tasks and reclaim planning time.
Mindful Tech
Digital wellness strategies and boundary-setting tools to reduce cognitive load and prevent burnout.
Smarter Grading, Less Grading
Tools that cut grading time while preserving academic integrity.
11:00 – 11:20 PM
Tools for the Modern Faculty Experience
Your AI Research Assistant
Hands-on intro to Claude, ChatGPT & Gemini for faculty-specific scholarly use cases beyond the classroom.
Visual Storytelling for Scholars
AI tools for compelling conference presentations and research infographics.
Staying Current Without the Overwhelm
RSS tools, curated newsletters & AI digests to keep faculty informed — not overloaded.
Why This Showcase, Why Now?
Shift in Faculty Needs
Faculty increasingly need support beyond pedagogy — research productivity, publication pipelines, and personal sustainability matter just as much.
Inclusive by Design
Sessions are intentionally mixed: some tool-forward and AI-free, others AI-enhanced — meeting faculty wherever they are on the adoption curve.
